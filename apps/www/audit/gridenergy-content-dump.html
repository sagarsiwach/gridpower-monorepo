/configureDraft
Guided sizer (conversion endpoint). A 4-step qualitative wizard. By design it NEVER outputs price, payback, or kWh — it collects context and books a survey.
Steps
  • Step 1 — What are we powering?
  • Step 2 — How much should it back up?
  • Step 3 — Do you have solar?
  • Step 4 — Where, and who do we reach?
Outputs a product class + survey booking, not a quote. No backend wired — submission does not persist yet.
